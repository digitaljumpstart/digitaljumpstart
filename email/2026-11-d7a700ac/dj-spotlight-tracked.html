picking the right ad platform for B2B

the difference is intent

Google Ads shows your ad when someone is already searching for what you sell, so you capture demand that exists. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) reach people as they scroll, so you create demand. LinkedIn Ads let you reach decision-makers by job title, seniority, industry or company size, but clicks and leads usually cost more.

four questions before you spend anything

  • Do people search for what you sell?
  • Can you describe your buyer by job?
  • What is a new customer worth to you?
  • How long does it take to win a client?

For many small businesses, the answer is to start with one platform, prove it works, then add a second.

clicks but no enquiries?

The platform is rarely the only problem. Common causes include sending traffic to the home page, no conversion tracking, targeting that's too broad, a slow website and no follow-up.
